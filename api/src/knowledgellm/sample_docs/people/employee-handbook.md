# Northstar People — Employee Handbook & Workplace Culture

> Fictional sample for public demonstration; not real company data or policy.

## 1. Welcome & Company Culture
Welcome to Northstar! We believe in fostering an inclusive, high-trust environment where distributed collaboration and creative problem-solving thrive.

## 2. Flexible & Hybrid Work Policy
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: 10:00 to 15:00 in your primary timezone to facilitate cross-functional meetings.
- **Remote Work**: Team members may work remotely, from Northstar hub offices, or in a hybrid arrangement with manager alignment.
- **Home Office Allowance**: A one-time setup reimbursement of up to **500 credits** is provided for ergonomic desk equipment and peripherals.

## 3. Learning & Development (L&D) Benefit
- **Annual Education Budget**: Each full-time employee receives **1,000 credits per year** for professional courses, books, technical conferences, or certifications.
- **Submission Process**: Requests can be submitted via the internal People Portal with simple manager sign-off.

## 4. Hardware & IT Security Standards
- Laptops are refreshed every **36 months**.
- Mandatory full-disk encryption (FileVault / BitLocker) and 1Password password manager usage.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all corporate tools and email accounts.

## 5. Support & Ethics Helpline
If you encounter workplace disputes, safety concerns, or compliance questions, reach out confidentially to `people-help@northstar.internal`.

# Northstar People — Time Off & Leave Policy

> Fictional sample for public demonstration; not real company data or policy.

## 1. Overview
Northstar values rest and work-life balance. We provide comprehensive paid leave options to support personal wellness, family commitments, and unexpected emergencies.

## 2. Annual Vacation Leave
- **Entitlement**: Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid vacation** per calendar year (accruing at 1.67 days per month).
- **Carry-Over**: Up to **5 unused vacation days** may be rolled over into the first quarter of the following calendar year.
- **Notice Period**: Please submit vacation requests at least **5 business days in advance** for leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## 3. Sick & Wellness Leave
- **Entitlement**: **10 paid medical leave days** per year for illness, mental health, or preventative medical appointments.
- **Medical Certification**: A qualified doctor's note is required only for continuous medical absences exceeding **2 consecutive days**.

## 4. Parental & Family Care Leave
- **Primary Caregiver**: **16 weeks of fully paid leave** following childbirth, adoption, or foster placement.
- **Secondary Caregiver**: **6 weeks of fully paid leave** taken within the child's first 12 months.
- **Compassionate Leave**: Up to **5 paid days** for bereavement or serious family medical emergencies.

## 5. Public Holidays
Northstar observes all official public holidays according to your regional contract location. A regional holiday schedule is posted annually in the People portal.

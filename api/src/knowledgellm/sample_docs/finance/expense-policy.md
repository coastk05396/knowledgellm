# Northstar Finance — Corporate Expense Policy

## 1. Overview & Policy Scope
This policy outlines eligible business expenses for Northstar team members. All expenses must be legitimate, reasonable, and necessary business costs incurred on behalf of the company.

## 2. Receipt Requirements & Submission Window
- **Mandatory Receipts**: Detailed itemized receipts are required for any single expense above **25 credits**.
- **Submission Deadline**: Expense reports must be submitted in the finance portal within **14 calendar days** of the transaction date.
- **Credit Card Reconciliation**: Monthly corporate card statements must be reconciled by the 5th of each following month.

## 3. Travel & Meal Guidelines
- **Meals Allowance**: Daily meal caps when traveling for business:
  - Breakfast: up to **20 credits**
  - Lunch: up to **35 credits**
  - Dinner: up to **65 credits**
- **Flights**: Economy class is mandatory for domestic and regional flights under 6 hours. Business class requires VP pre-approval.
- **Lodging**: Standard business hotel cap is **200 credits/night**. Bookings must be made at least 14 days in advance.

## 4. Approval Thresholds
- **Under 500 credits**: Direct manager approval required.
- **500 to 2,500 credits**: Department Head / Director approval required.
- **Above 2,500 credits**: VP of Finance sign-off required.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company does not reimburse personal grooming, traffic/parking violations, flight upgrades, or personal leisure entertainment.

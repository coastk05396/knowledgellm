# Northstar Finance — Q4 Operating Budget Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
The Northstar Finance leadership team has finalized the revised Q4 operating budget following vendor contract negotiations and mid-year performance reviews. Total approved operational expenditure for the quarter is set at **108,000 credits**.

## 2. Department Allocations
- **Engineering & Platform**: 45,000 credits (cloud compute, CI/CD runners, observability)
- **Marketing & Growth**: 25,000 credits (campaign ad spend, brand design, customer acquisition)
- **Operations & Support**: 18,000 credits (24/7 monitoring tools, incident response software)
- **People & Culture**: 12,000 credits (learning stipends, recruiting tools, team offsites)
- **General & Administrative**: 8,000 credits (legal compliance, insurance, accounting audits)

## 3. Contingency Reserve & Variance Analysis
- **Contingency Reserve**: An approved reserve of **8,000 credits** is held for unexpected vendor increases or emergency infrastructure scaling.
- **Licensing Variance**: SaaS software licensing increased by **3,200 credits** (+7.2%) compared to Q3.
- **Savings Realized**: Enterprise contract consolidation achieved **4,500 credits** in annualized savings.

## 4. Headcount & Planning Adjustments
- New headcount additions originally scheduled for late Q4 have been strategically shifted to the Q1 planning cycle.
- Discretionary contractor spend must receive pre-clearance from Finance before contract execution.
